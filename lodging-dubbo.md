# Lodging — Dubbo, NSW

Research note, 30 Aug 2026. Dubbo is the **best-equipped town in the path outside Sydney**, and on
the current reading it is the strongest middle option: nearly the same totality as Bourke, a real
airport, real hospitals, and roughly a hundred places to stay.

Sits alongside [Bourke](lodging-bourke.md), [the Central West](lodging-central-west.md), and the
full inventory in [`options.md`](options.md).

## The basics

| Cloud (July) | ~35–40% |
| Population | 40,578 (2021 urban locality); ~44,963 in postcode 2830 |
| Airport | 5 km from CBD, **24 h, no curfew** |
| Airlines | QantasLink, Rex, Link Airways, Air Link |
| Sydney flights | **~51 weekly departures** DBO–SYD |
| Also direct to | Brisbane, Melbourne, Broken Hill, Walgett, Lightning Ridge |

⚠️ Sources disagree slightly on duration: [australianeclipse](https://australianeclipse.com/cities/)
gives **3 m 50 s at 1:57 p.m.**; timeanddate gives **3 m 53 s, max 13:57:20**. A three-second
spread that changes nothing, but worth knowing the figures are not identical.

**This is the key access fact:** Dubbo is the only inland candidate where the group can *fly* from
Sydney rather than drive. For four travellers in their sixties-plus, a 1-hour flight instead of a
5-hour drive after a 14-hour international leg is not a small difference.

## Options for six people in three couples

### 1. Cattleman's Country Motor Inn & Serviced Apartments — the practical default

Dubbo's **largest property at ~170 rooms**, with **14+ room types including two-bedroom suites and
family rooms**, three pools (one indoor heated), and an award-winning restaurant on site.

Why it fits: it is the single property most likely to have three rooms together, or two
two-bedroom suites, and having a restaurant on site matters on a night when the whole town's
dining is under pressure. The indoor heated pool is a genuine plus in a Central West July.

### 2. A whole house — best value and best for a group

Dubbo has **75+ holiday rentals**. Named examples: **Palmer House** (3 bed / 2 bath), **Arthur
Abode** (3 bed, central), and **DBO Apartments**, a local operator with **studios through to
four-bedroom homes**, all central.

A three- or four-bedroom house is the natural shape for three couples: everyone under one roof,
one kitchen, no negotiating six breakfasts in a booked-out town. Usually cheaper than three rooms,
too. **This is my recommendation for this group.**

### 3. Serviced apartments — the middle ground

**Quest Dubbo** (near the train station) and **Manera Heights Apartments** offer self-contained
units with kitchens and laundry — hotel reliability with house flexibility. **Dubbo Apartments** is
another local self-contained operator.

### 4. Conventional motels — simple and plentiful

**Quality Inn Dubbo International** (beside the convention centre), **Nightcap at Macquarie Inn**,
**Bluegum Dubbo** (~23 rooms, 4.5-star boutique), plus a long tail of motels. Roughly a hundred
properties in total, so three rooms is not a hard ask — *if booked before the region tightens.*

## The distinctive option: staying inside Taronga Western Plains Zoo

Dubbo's zoo offers on-site accommodation, which nowhere else in the path can match. Three products:

| | What it is | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zoofari Lodge** | Safari-style lodges beside the savannah — giraffes, rhinos, zebra, antelope | **$309 per adult per night** |
| **Savannah Cabins** | Self-contained cabins just outside the zoo grounds | **$388 per cabin per night** (2 adults + 2 children), **2-night minimum** outside school holidays |
| **Billabong Camp** | Permanent tents near Lion Pride Lands. Each tent sleeps up to 3; groups of 4+ get two tents side by side, not shared with strangers | **$169 per adult per night** |

The Zoofari package includes two-day zoo admission, two exclusive guided tours, dinner, breakfast, a
day's bike hire and a 10% shop discount. Lodges have private ensuites, minibar, fridge,
air-conditioning and tea/coffee — and deliberately **no TV or phone**, with wifi only at the Guest
House.

### ⚠️ The catch — read this before assuming it works

The Bushland Lodges are advertised as sleeping **up to six**, which sounds perfect. The
configuration is **one queen bed, two singles and a sofa bed** — a *family* layout. That is one
double bed for three couples. **It does not work for this group as a single booking.**

Three couples at the zoo means **three separate units**, which changes the economics substantially:
at $309 per adult per night, six adults is roughly **$1,850 a night** before anything else.

Also unresolved on the public pages, and worth asking about directly:

- **Accessibility** — no mobility or step-free information is published. For safari-style canvas
  lodges and bush camping this is not a detail to assume.
- **Minimum stays** — the Savannah Cabins' two-night minimum lifts during school holidays, and
  **NSW winter school-holiday dates for 2028 need checking** against 22 July.
- Contact: **02 6881 1488** / **stayovernight@zoo.nsw.gov.au**

Verdict: a wonderful place to stay for a night or two of the trip. Probably not where you want to
be on eclipse morning, when what you want is to be free to drive.

## Demand outlook

Dubbo sits inside the Central West block that councils are already preparing for — the region
expects to sell out, with Bathurst positioning itself as the focal point and Ray Pickard estimating
100,000+ visitors even on his own halved figure. See
[`lodging-central-west.md`](lodging-central-west.md).

Dubbo's advantage in that competition is that it is **not** the focal point. Bathurst has the
observatory, the events programme and the publicity. Dubbo has more rooms and less attention —
which usually means it books out later and cheaper. Do not read that as safe: it is 100 properties
against a national and international audience, and the zoo accommodation in particular will go
early.

## How Dubbo compares

| | Sydney | **Dubbo** | Bourke | Kununurra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Totality | 3m48s | **3m50s** | 4m06s | ~5m |
| Cloud | ~40–47% | **~35–40%** | ~30% | **<10%** |
| Beds | ~26,000 CBD | ~100 properties | ~150–200 total | Moderate |
| Fly in? | International | **Yes, ~51/wk** | No | Yes, domestic |
| Hospital | Excellent | **Regional** | Small | Small |

**Dubbo's cloud gain over Sydney is real but modest** — a handful of percentage points for a
5-hour drive or a 1-hour flight. It buys you two other things that may matter more:

1. **A two-way escape route.** Dubbo sits mid-path with sealed highway running both east (Orange,
   Bathurst, Katoomba, Sydney) and west (toward Bourke) — all inside totality. From Sydney you can
   only run west.
2. **Far less competition than Bathurst**, with better air access than anywhere else inland.

## What to do now

1. **Price a three- or four-bedroom whole house** — the best fit for three couples, and Dubbo has
   the stock.
2. **Call Cattleman's** about holding two two-bedroom suites or three rooms for 21–23 July 2028,
   and ask when 2028 opens.
3. **Call the zoo (02 6881 1488)** if the group likes the idea — ask about three units, accessibility,
   and 2028 minimum stays. It will sell out first.
4. **Check flights, not just drives.** ~51 weekly Sydney services makes a fly-in trip realistic;
   confirm the July 2028 schedule when it loads, ~Aug 2027.
5. **Book the night before *and* after.** Nobody drives or flies out tired on eclipse evening.

## The honest recommendation

**Dubbo is the strongest inland-NSW option for this group, and the best middle path overall.**

It gives up ~16 seconds to Bourke and gains an airport, a regional hospital, a hundred properties
and a two-way escape route. It gives up ~70 minutes of cloud-odds advantage to Kununurra and saves
a long, expensive domestic leg. If the group wants better-than-Sydney odds without an expedition,
this is the answer.

What it does not do is move the odds much: ~35–40% against Sydney's ~40–47% is a real but modest
gain. **The decision between Sydney and Dubbo is mostly about trip shape, not about weather.** The
decision that actually moves the weather is Kununurra.
