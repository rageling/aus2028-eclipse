# Lodging — Bourke, NSW

Research note, 30 Aug 2026. Bourke is the **longest-totality town on the Australian mainland list
(4 min 6 s)**, which is why it keeps coming up. This page is what actually exists there to sleep in.

> ⚠️ **Nothing here is bookable yet.** Small outback properties typically open their calendars
> 12–18 months ahead, so July 2028 is not yet loadable at most of them. The action now is to
> **contact properties directly and get on a list**, not to book online.

## The capacity problem, first

| | Bourke | Dubbo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Town population (2021 census) | **1,699** | ~38,000 |
| Largest single property | ~26–30 rooms (Darling River Motel) | ~170 rooms (Cattleman's) |
| Total properties | ~12, all small | 100+ |
| Air service | Dubbo–Bourke only (Air Link, ~1h20m) | 4 airlines, ~51 weekly flights to Sydney |
| Totality | **4 min 6 s** | 3 min 50 s |

A town of seventeen hundred people, with roughly 150–200 beds all told, holds the longest
city-centre totality in the country. That will be discovered by the global eclipse-chasing
community. **Bourke is the single most likely town on the whole NSW track to be fully booked
years out.**

## The options

Sourced from the [official Bourke tourism site](https://www.visitbourke.com.au/stay) and
[VisitNSW](https://www.visitnsw.com/destinations/outback-nsw/bourke-area/bourke/accommodation).
Room counts where found are noted; **treat them as approximate** — published figures conflict.

### Best fit for six people in three couples

**Self-contained apartments — the strongest option.**

| Property | Type | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Browns Apartments** | Two-bedroom, self-contained | Two units cover all six with a spare bedroom. Kitchen and shared living space — the group eats together instead of hunting for a restaurant that can seat six in a town this size |
| **Ann's Apartments** | Two-bedroom furnished units | Same shape; near the main shopping area |

For a multi-generational group this beats separate motel rooms: shared space matters over a week,
and self-catering removes a real constraint — Bourke's dining options are limited and eclipse week
will strain them.

| Property | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Mount Oxley / Rossmore Station** | Working-station stay. Possible exclusive use for a group of six — worth asking |
| **Mays Bend** | Historic river property, former stock crossing |

Station stays offer something the motels cannot: genuinely dark skies and space to set up without a
crowd. The trade is distance from medical care and rougher amenities — a real consideration for
four travellers in their sixties-plus.

## Getting there

Bourke is **~800 km northwest of Sydney**, roughly a 9–10 hour drive. Options:

- **Drive from Sydney** — a two-day trip realistically, not one, with this group.
- **Fly Sydney→Dubbo, then drive** — ~4 hours Dubbo to Bourke on sealed road. The practical route.
- **Fly Sydney→Dubbo→Bourke** — Air Link began a Dubbo–Bourke service (~1h20m) in March 2026.
  Tiny aircraft, thin schedule; verify it still operates before relying on it, and do not assume it
  scales for eclipse week.

## What to do now

1. **Phone the two apartment operators and the two largest motels directly.** Ask when 2028
   calendars open and whether they keep a waitlist. Email leaves a record; phone gets an answer.
2. **Ask about accessibility explicitly** — ground-floor rooms, step-free access, bathroom rails.
   Older outback motel stock is frequently single-storey, which helps, but confirm rather than assume.
3. **Ask about minimum-stay requirements.** Eclipse-week three- and four-night minimums are near
   certain, and standard practice for events like this.
4. **Expect eclipse-week pricing.** Budget several times the ~AUD 100–150/night ordinary rate.

## The honest recommendation

**Bourke's advantage over Dubbo is 16 seconds.**

That is the whole duration difference — 4:06 against 3:50. Bourke's cloud odds are modestly better
(~30% against ~35–40%), which is a real but not decisive edge. Against that, Dubbo has a hundred-plus
properties, a 170-room hotel, four airlines and roughly 51 flights a week from Sydney, and a proper
regional hospital.

For a group of six including four older travellers, **Dubbo is very likely the better version of the
inland-NSW play**, with Bourke as an eclipse-morning drive west only if the forecast demands it —
though note that is a 4-hour drive, so it is a day-before decision, not a morning one.

Bourke is the right answer only if the group is willing to book in early 2027 the week the decision
is made, and to accept small-town amenities in exchange for the best cloud odds available without
leaving New South Wales.

This does not change the standing recommendation in [`site-selection.md`](site-selection.md) — it
sharpens the inland-NSW branch of it.
